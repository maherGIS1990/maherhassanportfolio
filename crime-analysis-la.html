Crime Analysis in Los Angeles

A comprehensive spatial analysis of crime patterns in Los Angeles, exploring temporal trends, hotspots, and the distribution of different crime types across districts.

Project objective

The primary goal of this project was to visualize and analyze crime data in Los Angeles to identify high-risk areas, understand the spatial distribution of specific crimes like theft, and observe how crime levels changed over time.

Methodology

  • Collected and cleaned crime data for Los Angeles
  • Mapped spatial distribution of all crimes across the city
  • Performed hexbin analysis for 2020 to identify density patterns
  • Analyzed specific crime types like theft and their locations
  • Evaluated crime distribution across different city districts
  • Compared crime trends from 2020 through 2022

Key outcome

The analysis revealed significant spatial clusters of criminal activity, allowing for a better understanding of neighborhood safety and providing insights that could assist in strategic resource allocation.
